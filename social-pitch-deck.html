The Brief

02 · Who this is for

Yummers is built for parents and caregivers aged 25 to 45 raising kids from 6 months to 13 years old — working professionals, stay-at-home parents, and single parents, mostly in urban or suburban households. They care about convenience and healthy eating, but they're up against picky eaters, lunchbox fatigue, and food allergies. They trust expert advice, especially from pediatric nutritionists, and they're open to a subscription that makes family life a little easier.
